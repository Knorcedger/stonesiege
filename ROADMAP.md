# StoneSiege roadmap

StoneSiege is in internal alpha. This roadmap communicates direction, not guaranteed dates. Issues and measured playtests determine the order within each stage.

## Now — make the single-player alpha excellent

- Open the repository and make contribution paths welcoming
- Complete device QA across representative Android phones, iPhones, and tablets
- Fix campaign blockers, input friction, safe-area issues, and accessibility gaps
- Measure and improve battle performance, memory use, load time, and bundle size
- Validate balance across the full unit-counter web
- Improve onboarding, settings, help, and recovery from interrupted matches
- Promote stable builds from internal testing toward public testing

## Next — deepen the free single-player game

- More maps, scenarios, and campaign content
- Better AI personalities, scouting, difficulty tuning, and team behavior
- Replays and a player-facing save/load flow
- Formations, stances, richer terrain, and additional civilizations
- Community translation and accessibility infrastructure
- Supported mod/data workflows where they can remain safe and deterministic

## Later — investigate fair multiplayer

- Prototype deterministic lockstep networking and desync diagnosis
- Threat-model accounts, matchmaking, abuse prevention, privacy, and operating cost
- Run small community tests before committing to a commercial service
- If viable, ship official multiplayer as a separate one-time purchase

Multiplayer is not promised until its security, reliability, and ongoing costs are understood. No ads, subscriptions, loot boxes, or pay-to-win systems are planned.

## Not currently planned

- Advertising or behavior-based tracking
- Energy timers, randomized paid rewards, or consumable power
- A paid gate around the existing single-player game
- Blockchain, tokens, or play-to-earn systems

To propose a change, open a feature request and follow the decision process in [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md).
