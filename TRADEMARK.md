# StoneSiege name and brand policy

The open-source licenses in this repository grant rights to code and certain creative assets. They do not grant trademark rights.

The **StoneSiege** name, crest, logos, app icon, and official store identity identify releases maintained by the StoneSiege project. The maintainers reserve those identifiers to protect players from confusing or unsafe unofficial builds.

## You may

- Truthfully say that your work is based on, compatible with, or derived from StoneSiege.
- Link to the official project and use the name in ordinary factual discussion.
- Say “for StoneSiege” for a mod, tool, guide, or community project, provided the presentation does not imply endorsement.
- Use unmodified screenshots for commentary, reviews, tutorials, or reporting where otherwise legally permitted.

## Please ask first

- Using a StoneSiege logo or crest as the primary identity of a community group, event, domain, social account, product, or service.
- Selling merchandise with the name, crest, or logo.
- Using the brand in a way that could reasonably look like an official release or partnership.

## Forks and redistributed builds

Forks are welcome. Before distributing one, choose a clearly distinct product name, replace the StoneSiege icon and logos, and state that the build is unofficial. Do not use `com.stonesiege.app` or another identifier that suggests it is the official mobile application.
