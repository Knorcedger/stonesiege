# StoneSiege asset license

StoneSiege uses a split license so that the game can be meaningfully forked without allowing a fork to present itself as the official StoneSiege release.

## Open game assets

Except for the brand assets and third-party materials described below, the original visual art, generated atlases, animation frames, scenario prose, sound design, and other non-code game content in this repository are dedicated to the public domain under [CC0 1.0 Universal](LICENSE-ASSETS) (**CC0 1.0**; [canonical page](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)).

This includes the original material under:

- `art/hd/`
- `apps/web/public/assets/`
- `docs/ART_BIBLE.md`
- `docs/ASSET_CONTRACT.md`
- `docs/CAMPAIGN_WALLACE.md`
- `art/store/screenshots/`

To the extent that a file contains material for which no copyright or similar right exists, no restriction is asserted. To the extent that the project or a contributor holds copyright and related rights, they are waived under CC0, with CC0's public-license fallback applying where a waiver is not legally effective.

Attribution is appreciated but not required. Suggested credit:

> Contains public-domain assets from the StoneSiege project and its contributors. Changes were made.

## Brand assets

The following are official identity assets and are **not** dedicated under CC0:

- `assets/app-icon-source.png`
- `assets/app-icon-mark.png`
- `assets/logo.png`
- `art/play-store-icon.png`
- `art/store/feature-graphic.jpg`
- native app icons derived from those files under `android/` and `ios/`

The StoneSiege name, crest, logos, and official store presentation are governed by [TRADEMARK.md](TRADEMARK.md). Forks should replace these assets and choose a distinct name before distribution.

## AI-assisted material

Some source art was created with OpenAI image-generation tools and then selected, edited, matted, sliced, recolored, or composed through the repository's deterministic art pipeline. Provenance and production notes live in `art/hd/README.md` and relevant source directories.

Contributors must disclose material AI assistance and may submit only material they reasonably believe they have the right to dedicate under CC0. Do not submit recognizable third-party characters, logos, artwork, or close copies of another game's assets.

## Third-party material

Dependencies are not relicensed by this project. The bundled Fontsource packages contain fonts under their upstream SIL Open Font License terms; see their package metadata and license files. Other dependencies retain the licenses declared by their respective authors.

If a file carries its own license notice, that notice controls for that file. If you find an attribution or provenance mistake, report it privately to [support@flarmio.com](mailto:support@flarmio.com) or open a narrowly scoped issue that does not reproduce disputed material.
